# Thai Streaming Platforms — Checklist

> Use this as a **list of places to check** only — it is not evidence that any title is or is not available.
> The streaming market changes quickly (shutdowns, mergers, renames) — review this list every 3 months.
> Last reviewed: YYYY-MM-DD (not yet verified online — verify before first real use)

## Aggregators (check first)
| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| JustWatch Thailand (`justwatch.com/th`) | Shows which Thai platforms carry a title, split by Stream / Rent / Buy / Free |

## Platforms to check
| Platform | Main type | Focus | How to verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netflix | Subscription | Movies / series / anime, several tiers | Title page `netflix.com/th/title/...` |
| Prime Video | Subscription / Rent / Buy | Movies / series | Title page, Thailand region |
| Disney+ Hotstar | Subscription | Marvel, Star Wars, Disney | Title page, Thailand region |
| HBO Max | Subscription | HBO, Warner Bros. | Title page, Thailand region |
| Apple TV+ / Apple TV app | Subscription / Rent / Buy | Apple Originals + rental store | Title page, Thailand region |
| Google TV / YouTube Movies | Rent / Buy | Movie rentals and purchases | Title page, Thailand region |
| Viu | Subscription / Free (ads) | Asian series | Title page, Thailand region |
| iQIYI | Subscription / Free | Chinese/Asian series, some anime | Title page, Thailand region |
| WeTV | Subscription / Free | Chinese/Thai series | Title page, Thailand region |
| TrueID | Subscription / Free | Thai-licensed movies / anime | Title page |
| Bilibili (TH) | Free / Premium | Anime | Title page |
| Crunchyroll | Subscription / Free (ads) | Anime | Title page — confirm it serves the Thailand region |
| Official YouTube channels (e.g. Muse Asia, Ani-One Asia) | Free | Licensed anime | Must be the license holder's official channel |

> This table is a starting point, not a complete list. When a search reveals another legal platform serving Thailand,
> add a row with the date it was found.
