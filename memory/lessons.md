## Seed lessons
- Titles with multiple versions (One Piece, Attack on Titan, Death Note, etc.) → always ask back before searching
- Anime in Thailand is often listed under a Thai or localized title → search English, Thai, and romaji names
- Platform season numbering may differ from the original (e.g. a Part listed as a Season) → use what the platform shows and add a note if it differs
- JustWatch can lag behind the real catalog → open the platform page to confirm whenever possible
- Search results with no country stated are often US data → never use them for Thailand; mark `ไม่พบข้อมูล` and explain in notes
- "Not available in your country" on a platform page refers to the agent's own location, not Thailand → not evidence for Thailand
- "Could not find it" ≠ "not available" → use `ไม่พบข้อมูล` unless the platform page itself was checked
- Excel format must come from the script in SKILL.md → never write new Excel code by hand
- Movie series without a part (e.g. Harry Potter) → do not ask; search all films and show parts per platform (`ภาค 1–3`, `ภาค 2–7`). Harry Potter has 8 films, not 7 (Deathly Hallows is split into 2 films)
- One name, several different film series (e.g. Spider-Man) → ask which series first
- Links must open the platform's own title page; JustWatch / wheretowatch pages go in sources only
- Searching `รับชม "<Thai title>"` finds Thai platform title pages (Disney+ / HBO Max) that English searches miss
